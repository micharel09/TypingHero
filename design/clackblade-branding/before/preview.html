Run game

A sword duel played on your keyboard. Finish words to strike, read the skeleton's cue, and turn a well-timed parry into an opening.

Free in your desktop browser. Keyboard required. Click Run game above to play.

Type to attack

Complete the highlighted word to swing your sword.

Space to parry

Watch for the yellow attack cue and time Space to parry the incoming swing.

Unleash Slayer

Break enemy stamina to unleash Slayer: each correct keystroke becomes a rapid strike.

Learn the rhythm

The interactive tutorial walks through attacking, accuracy, parrying and Slayer. Skip it or replay it from the menu.
