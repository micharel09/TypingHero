TYPE TO ATTACK. SPACE TO PARRY. BREAK STAMINA TO UNLEASH SLAYER.

Finish the word before the skeleton's next swing. Watch its attack cue, time Space to parry, then turn the opening into another sword strike. Break enemy stamina and unleash Slayer: correct keystrokes become rapid attacks.

Play now: click Run game above. Free in your desktop browser, with a keyboard. No download required.

A sword duel played on your keyboard

  • Type to attack: complete the highlighted word to swing your sword.
  • Space to parry: read the enemy's cue and time your defense while keeping up with the words.
  • Break stamina for Slayer: hits and parries build toward a burst of rapid strikes driven by correct keystrokes.
  • Chase a better run: keep your accuracy, build a combo, and compare scores on the global Top 1000.

Learn the rhythm

The interactive tutorial walks through attacking, accuracy, parrying and Slayer. You can skip it or replay it from the menu.

Controls: letters to attack, Space to parry, Esc to pause. Use R to restart or M to return to the menu from pause or results.
