# Type to attack. Space to parry. What happens when stamina breaks?

CLACKBLADE is a sword duel played on your keyboard. Completing a word swings your sword; timing Space against the next attack keeps you alive. Hits and parries wear down enemy stamina. Break it, and Slayer turns correct keystrokes into rapid strikes.

I've updated the game page to make that loop easier to see: a keyboard-focused cover, clearer controls and gameplay images. The game still plays free in a desktop browser with a keyboard.
