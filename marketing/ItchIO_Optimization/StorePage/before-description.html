Type to slash. Press Space to parry. Chase the high score.

Face a sword-wielding skeleton in a 2D pixel-art duel. Finish the words on screen to attack, then time your parries to stay alive. Break the skeleton's stamina to enter Slayer mode and turn correct keystrokes into a flurry of strikes.

Play free in your desktop browser. You need a keyboard; no download is required.

How to play

  • Type the highlighted word to swing your sword.
  • Press Space as the enemy attacks to parry.
  • Break enemy stamina with hits and parries to trigger Slayer mode. Type quickly and accurately during the opening.
  • Press Esc to pause. From the pause or end screen, press R to restart or M to return to the menu.

Set your best score

Enter a player name, build your combo and see how far you can climb on the global Top 1000 leaderboard. Open Top 1000 from the menu or pause screen. Try another run and beat your personal best.

Development

Typing Hero is in active development. If you encounter a problem, please leave a comment with your browser, what you were doing and what happened. Gameplay feedback is welcome too.
