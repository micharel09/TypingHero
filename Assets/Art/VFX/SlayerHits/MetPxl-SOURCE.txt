User-approved adaptation of the existing B preview, 2026-10-25.
Only the white tile (640,640)-(896,896) is used. All six frames contain only
black/white RGB; their 4x blocks reduce exactly to native 64x64 pixels.
Black becomes alpha 0; white becomes alpha 255. RGB remains white throughout.
No interpolation, generated pixels, altered silhouette or reconstructed motion.
Source cels are stored F0-F5 left to right in metpxl-white.png (384x64 RGBA).
Playback: F4 80ms, F5/F0/F1/F2/F3 100ms each; once per confirmed Slayer hit.
