Six-page tutorial: whole 16:9 gameplay captures from the normal player.
Each source PNG is converted to RGBA and resized once to 320x180 with
nearest-neighbor. No cropping, HUD composition, masking or component movement.
Point-filtered atlases: six columns, at most eleven rows, two-pixel gutters,
maximum 2048 pixels. Chronological source durations and repeated frames remain.

Matching JSON manifests record source files, original timestamps, durations,
source/build paths, source hashes, resized RGBA hashes, atlas hashes and events.
The reproducible packer is tools/game_loop/tutorial_pack.py.
